# nasa-space-challenge-2026
     ASTRA
Space Mission Design & Exploration Simulator

NASA International Space Apps Challenge 2026

     About the Project

ASTRA is an educational and scientific simulation game that allows players to design, plan, manage, and execute space exploration missions across multiple celestial bodies.

Rather than focusing on a single destination, ASTRA provides a platform for exploring different worlds such as the Moon, Mars, asteroids, icy moons, and future extraterrestrial environments. The game's architecture is designed to support missions on any planetary body while adapting to its specific environmental and scientific challenges.

The project combines mission engineering, scientific exploration, resource management, and immersive 3D exploration into a single interactive experience.

     Challenge Addressed

Space missions are often taught through isolated concepts:

Energy systems
Communications
Scientific instruments
Mission planning
Planetary exploration
Vehicle design

ASTRA bridges these disciplines by demonstrating how every mission decision affects every other component of the mission. Success depends on balancing scientific goals, available resources, environmental constraints, mission duration, and operational risks.

     Our Solution

ASTRA combines two complementary layers:

1. Mission Design & Management

Players prepare and configure missions before launch:

Select mission objectives
Choose scientific instruments
Manage energy budgets
Allocate resources
Optimize payload mass
Plan exploration routes
Define mission strategies

Every decision influences mission capabilities and outcomes.

2. Immersive Planetary Exploration

Once deployed, players explore a fully interactive environment using:

An astronaut
Exploration vehicles (rovers)
Scientific instruments
Mapping tools
Mission control interfaces

Players investigate locations of scientific interest, collect samples, perform observations, and complete mission objectives.

     Educational Objectives

Resource management
Mission planning
Scientific decision-making

Engineering trade-offs
Data acquisition and transmission
